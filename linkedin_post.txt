🔭 I Built a Tool That Turns Math Into Something You Can Actually See

A few weeks ago, I came across an Instagram reel showing a beautiful 3D mathematical surface spinning on screen. It looked like digital art — but it was created entirely from an equation.

That single reel sparked an idea.

I wondered: **Why can't students and learners explore mathematics like this directly in their browser, for free?**

So I built **NOVA MathPlot** — an interactive scientific visualization platform that makes mathematics more visual, intuitive, and engaging.

🌐 Live Demo: [NOVA MathPlot](https://nova-mathplot.vercel.app?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
💻 GitHub: [NOVA MathPlot on GitHub](https://github.com/Hafiz-Sakib/nova-mathplot?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 🚀 What NOVA MathPlot Can Do

📈 **2D Function Plotter**
Enter expressions like `sin(x)`, `x² - 3x`, or `e^(-x²)` and see them plotted instantly. You can visualize multiple functions at the same time.

🧊 **3D Surface Visualizer**
Explore more than 60 mathematical structures, including the Lorenz Attractor, Klein Bottle, Möbius Strip, and DNA Helix, with real-time rotation and zoom.

🌀 **Parametric Curves**
Watch elegant curves such as Lissajous figures, Euler spirals, and torus knots animate themselves in real time.

ℂ **Complex Analysis**
Visualize the real part, imaginary part, and magnitude of complex-valued functions simultaneously.

🧠 **Neural Activation Explorer**
Study 17 activation functions used in machine learning, including ReLU, Sigmoid, GELU, and Mish, along with their derivatives.

## ✨ Key Features

* 350+ example mathematical functions
* 60+ 3D presets
* 40+ parametric curve presets
* 17 neural network activation functions
* Premium dark and light themes
* Fully client-side with no backend required
